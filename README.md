# SIH26090-Artisan-Market-Linkage

## AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging for Marginalized Artisans

SIH26090 is an AI-powered platform designed to help marginalized artisans digitize their products, create professional marketplace-ready listings, connect with buyers, and maintain product provenance and transparency.

The platform is designed around a simple principle:

> **One product. One voice. Multiple markets.**

---

## Problem

Many artisans face barriers in:

- Creating professional product listings
- Writing digital catalog descriptions
- Accessing online markets
- Communicating in non-English languages
- Establishing craft and GI-related information
- Maintaining product provenance
- Understanding pricing and payment transparency

Our solution aims to reduce these barriers through an accessible, AI-assisted digital workflow.
